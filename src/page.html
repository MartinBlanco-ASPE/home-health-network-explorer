How to read this view

Methods, cutoff and interpretation limits

Clusters group wording in the displayed connections; they are not the predefined flag families, validated themes, provider ties or patient-level joint events. Source condition hierarchy and screening flags are separate. Contexts are selected survey records, not comparable random populations. Flags are review prompts, not actual harm or IJ determinations.

Reading the map

Methods and limits

The corpus contains 54,377 candidate care/organizational records from 6,122 source CCNs. All 607 held records and the E stream remain excluded. Survey groups are exact CCN/event/date keys, not independently validated incidents. The workbook has no dedicated accreditor, structured IJ or report-URL field.

Actual reading coverage

One AI-assisted pass covers all 48 proposed complete care accounts from 24 source CCNs, plus their cover memos. Other source examples are automatically selected illustrations. There is no independent human semantic validation, representative sampling or saturation claim.

Provisional severity review

Eight care-obligation families screen explicit deficit wording. Their equal-weight, capped family count measures screening breadth; it is not a clinical severity scale. Source policies anchor the obligation, not the numeric score. Quoted requirements, missing documentation and clinical background can affect results. Historical applicability is not fully reconciled.
